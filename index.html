🪔 शुभ धनतेरस 🪔

HAPPY DHANTERAS

May Goddess Lakshmi bless your life with endless wealth, good health, happiness, success, peace, and prosperity.

तपाईंको जीवनमा सुख, समृद्धि, धन-सम्पत्ति, ऐश्वर्य, वैभव र आनन्दको वर्षा होस्।

May this Dhanteras bring endless prosperity, happiness, and divine blessings into your life.

शुभ धनतेरस! मंगलमय जीवन!
